I'm Nathan. I work on software that sits close to the metal - container runtimes, kernels,
embedded video, and the occasional machine-learning experiment built from scratch to see how
a thing actually works.

Most of what I know, I learned by building it. That habit is what this blog is: write-ups
where I work through a problem in public, including the parts that don't work the first time.

## What you'll find here

- **[Writing](/posts/)** - mostly systems and compilers, with maths and machine learning mixed in.
- **[Portfolio](/projects/)** - projects I've built and the skills behind them.
- **[Research](/research/)** - longer notes that haven't become posts yet.
