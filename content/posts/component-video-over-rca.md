## Introduction

An [RCA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCA_connector) connector is used to carry analog video and audio signals and it stands for 
**Radio Corporation of America**. It's ubiquitous and can be found in DVD players,amplifiers,
radio receivers e.t.c


## Composite Video
* Also known as  CVBS(Composite Video Baseband Signal) or (Color Video Blanking Sync).
* It combines image information such as luminance,chrominance and synchronization into a single signal
* The signal is transmitted over one channnel
